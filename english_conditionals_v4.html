What is a conditional sentence?

A conditional sentence expresses a relationship between a condition and a result. It typically has an if-clause (the condition) and a main clause (the result). English has six main conditional types, each expressing a different degree of reality, possibility, or time reference.

This lesson also covers conditional questions (e.g. "What would you do if…?") and conditional inversion (e.g. "Should you need help…", "Had I known…"), which appear in formal English.
