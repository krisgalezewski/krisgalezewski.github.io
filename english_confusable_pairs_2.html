English Confusable Pairs
The words and structures that look similar, feel similar — but mean something completely different
Why confusable pairs matter

Even advanced learners make systematic errors with pairs like do / make, since / for, or mustn't / don't have to. These are not vocabulary problems — they are system problems. Learning the underlying logic of each pair removes dozens of errors at once.
