What this lesson covers

English has 12 tenses formed by combining three time frames — past, present, and future — with four aspects: simple, continuous, perfect, and perfect continuous. Each combination expresses a distinct relationship between an action and time.

Aspects
The four aspects
Simple
Completed or habitual actions
Continuous
Actions in progress / temporary
Perfect
Actions with relevance to another time
Perfect continuous
Duration up to a point in time
