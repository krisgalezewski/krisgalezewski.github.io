How English builds new words — parts of speech, prefixes, suffixes, word families, and the patterns that connect them
Why word formation matters

Understanding how words are built gives you a superpower: when you meet an unknown word, you can decode its meaning from its parts. When you know one word in a family, you can often generate related forms without memorising each one separately. If you know create, you get creation, creative, creatively, creativity, creator, recreate — six words for the price of one.
