What is inversion?

Inversion means placing the auxiliary verb before the subject. This usually happens after negative or restrictive adverbials in formal English.

Sentence structure
Negative adverbial auxiliary subject main verb rest of sentence
Examples
Never had she seen such chaos.
Rarely do students understand inversion immediately.
Only after the meeting did he apologise.
Remember: the auxiliary verb moves before the subject. If there is no auxiliary in the original sentence, use do / does / did.
Common mistakes
NormalShe rarely visits the office.
Correct inversionRarely does she visit the office.
IncorrectRarely she visits the office.
